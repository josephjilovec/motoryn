## Generation brief

Photorealistic-natural premium automotive editorial photo for Motoryn, an independent vehicle maintenance planning concept. Rear three-quarter side view of an unbranded elegant graphite blue everyday sport wagon in a clean understated independent service garage, warm side light and a single subtle red workshop accent, realistic tires, correct wheels, tidy concrete, no people. Wide landscape 3:2 cinematic composition with beautiful paint reflections and restrained dark mood. Do not show manufacturer emblems, readable plate text, brand names, lettering or watermark. Attainable ownership rather than supercars.
